Make a 3D night-city street racing game with three.js. Rain-slick asphalt reflecting neon signs, glowing storefronts, steam vents, traffic you weave through, and a car with headlights, drift and a nitro boost. Include three laps around a city circuit, a lap timer, a minimap, engine and tire sounds, and a finish screen with your best time. Focus on detail and atmosphere.

---
Operational notes (harness, not part of the brief):

* Work in the current directory. Name the deliverable `racer.html` (openable in Chrome by double-clicking; inline JS/CSS or keep extra files next to it; libraries may come from a CDN such as cdnjs or jsdelivr).
* WRITE EARLY: as soon as anything renders, write `racer.html` and check it loads; then add detail step by step. A rough version on disk beats a detailed one you never wrote.
* LOOK AT WHAT YOU BUILT: render it headless or open it in your browser tool, read the console, and check the screenshot is not blank before you finish.
